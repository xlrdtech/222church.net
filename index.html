Founder & Lead Pastor

Meet Atiba Adams, M.A.

Atiba Adams is the founder of 222 Church and 222 Disciples, a Christ-centered discipleship movement established to help people discover their identity, purpose, and authority in Jesus Christ and become equipped to make disciples who make disciples.

His vision is rooted in 2 Timothy 2:2: entrust the truth of God's Word to faithful people who will be able to teach others also. Through teaching, mentorship, counseling and discipleship, he helps believers grow beyond attending services into mature disciples who live on mission.

Love God. Love people. Make disciples.
